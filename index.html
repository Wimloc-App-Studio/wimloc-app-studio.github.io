The studio

Apps born from family life

Wimloc App Studio is Thomas, a father. The idea is simple: when a need keeps coming back at home, a small app can solve it. A game to keep the children busy in the car, a tool to organise the week…

Every app is designed to be simple, useful and respectful: no ads, no data sent, nothing unnecessary. And for children, always humour and tenderness, never fear or violence.
