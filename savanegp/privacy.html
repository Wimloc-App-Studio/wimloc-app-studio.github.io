Privacy policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

SavaneGP is a game for children published by Wimloc App Studio. It does not collect, store on a server or share any personal data.

  • The game works without an internet connection and sends no information.
  • There are no ads, no purchases, no account and no analytics.
  • Best times, cups and game settings are only stored on the phone. They are deleted when the game is uninstalled.
  • The game does not use the camera, the microphone, the location or the contacts.

Questions: wimlocappstudio@gmail.com
